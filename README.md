### Basic Documents Required for BIS Registration

Here is the complete list of documents generally required for BIS Registration in Noida:

1.  **Company Registration Proof**
    *   Certificate of Incorporation (for Private Limited / LLP)
    *   Partnership Deed (for partnership firms)
    *   Shop & Establishment License / Udyam Registration (for proprietorship)
2.  **GST Registration Certificate** A valid GST certificate with Noida or Uttar Pradesh GSTIN is mandatory.
3.  **PAN Card**
    *   PAN Card of the Company / LLP
    *   PAN Card of Proprietor / Partners / Directors
4.  **Address Proof of Registered Office & Factory**
    *   Rent Agreement / Ownership Deed
    *   Electricity Bill / Water Bill (recent)
    *   NOC from Landlord (if rented)
5.  **Authorized Signatory Documents**
    *   Board Resolution on company letterhead
    *   Authorization Letter
    *   ID Proof (Aadhaar / Passport / Voter ID) and Address Proof of authorized signatory
6.  **Product Details**
    *   Product specifications and technical data sheet
    *   Manufacturing process flow chart
    *   Product drawings / diagrams (if applicable)
    *   List of raw materials used
7.  **Test Reports**
    *   Test reports from BIS-recognized laboratories as per relevant Indian Standards (IS Code)
    *   For electronics products, test reports must cover all applicable parameters under CRS

### Additional Documents for ISI Mark Scheme

For products requiring factory inspection (ISI Mark), you will also need:

*   Factory layout plan with dimensions
*   List of manufacturing machinery and equipment
*   Quality Control Manual and testing equipment details
*   Details of technical personnel / competent technical staff
*   Calibration certificates of testing equipment
*   Undertaking on company letterhead for compliance

### Documents Required for CRS (Electronics Products)

For electronics and IT goods under Compulsory Registration Scheme, the focus is more on:

*   Product labelling and marking details
*   User manual and safety instructions
*   Critical components list with supplier details
*   PCB layout and schematics (in some cases)
*   Homologation or other regulatory approvals (if applicable)

Many electronics manufacturers in Noida specifically need **[BIS Certification for Electronics](https://www.agileregulatory.com/blogs/bis-certification-for-electronic-products)** and must submit detailed technical files.

### Documents Required for Foreign Manufacturers (FMCS)

Foreign companies applying under the Foreign Manufacturer Certification Scheme need extra documents such as:

*   Power of Attorney in favour of Authorized Indian Representative (AIR)
*   Details of AIR
*   Notarized documents from the country of origin
*   Embassy attestation (in some cases)

### Common Document Mistakes to Avoid in Noida

*   Mismatch in company name or address across documents
*   Expired GST or PAN
*   Poor quality scanned copies
*   Missing technical details in test reports
*   Incomplete factory layout plan
*   Not having competent technical staff details

These small errors can cause repeated queries and delays from BIS officials.
